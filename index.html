About Us / 關於我們

多文化大家庭：雙語敬拜、真誠團契、一起成長

Christ-centered • Multicultural • Bilingual

Our Community / 我們的社群

Chinese / 中文

我們是一個服務於聖路易 (St. Louis) 地區的多文化教會大家庭，並以中文與英文進行雙語交流。我們致力於建立一個以基督為中心、彼此相愛並互相尊重的環境。在這裡，不同的文化相遇，在基督的愛裡合而為一。

English

We are a multicultural church family serving the St. Louis area, united through our bilingual Chinese and English community. We are dedicated to building a Christ-centered environment where loving and respecting one another is at the heart of everything we do—a place where different cultures meet and become one in the love of Christ.

Worship • Love • Gospel

Our Vision / 我們的願景

Chinese / 中文

我們盼望成為一個以基督為中心的教會，敬拜神、彼此相愛，並向社區分享福音。無論你是第一次來教會，還是在尋找屬靈的家，我們都誠摯歡迎你加入我們，一同敬拜、成長。

English

We seek to be a Christ-centered church that worships God, loves one another, and shares the gospel with our community. Whether you are visiting for the first time or looking for a spiritual home, we warmly welcome you to join us as we worship and grow together.
